# 易混词辨析

## 一、动词辨析

### 1. affect vs effect

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| affect | v. | 影响 | The weather affects my mood. |
| effect | n. | 影响，效果 | The medicine had a good effect. |
| effect | v. | 实现，使发生 | effect a change（使变化发生） |

**记忆口诀**：affect是动词用a，effect名词用e

---

### 2. rise vs raise vs arise

| 词 | 用法 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| rise | vi.（rose, risen） | 上升，升起 | The sun rises in the east. |
| raise | vt.（raised, raised） | 举起，提高，养育 | Please raise your hand. / raise children |
| arise | vi.（arose, arisen） | 出现，产生 | Problems arose. |

**记忆口诀**：rise自己升，raise养育它，arise问题来

---

### 3. spend vs cost vs take vs pay

| 词 | 主语 | 结构 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| spend | 人 | spend time/money (in) doing / on sth | I spent 2 hours finishing the work. |
| cost | 物/事 | sth cost sb money | The book cost me 20 yuan. |
| take | it形式主语 | It takes sb time to do | It took me 2 hours to finish the work. |
| pay | 人 | pay (money) for sth | I paid 20 yuan for the book. |

---

### 4. borrow vs lend vs keep

| 词 | 含义 | 方向 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| borrow | 借入 | 别人→自己 | Can I borrow your pen? |
| lend | 借出 | 自己→别人 | Can you lend me your pen? |
| keep | 保留 | 用于表示借多长时间 | How long can I keep the book? |

**记忆口诀**：borrow进来，lend出去，keep表时间

---

### 5. remember/forget + to do vs doing

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| remember to do | 记得要做（未做） | Remember to lock the door. |
| remember doing | 记得做过（已做） | I remember locking the door. |
| forget to do | 忘记要做（未做） | I forgot to call him. |
| forget doing | 忘记做过（已做） | I forgot calling him. |

---

### 6. stop + to do vs doing

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| stop to do | 停下来去做（另一件事） | He stopped to smoke.（停下来抽烟） |
| stop doing | 停止做（正在做的事） | He stopped smoking.（戒烟） |

---

### 7. try + to do vs doing

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| try to do | 努力做（结果不一定成功） | I tried to open the door, but failed. |
| try doing | 尝试做（看效果如何） | I tried opening the door with a key. |

---

## 二、形容词/副词辨析

### 1. interested vs interesting

| 词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| interested | 人+be interested in | I am interested in English. |
| interesting | 物+is interesting | The book is interesting. |

**同类词**：
- excited / exciting（兴奋的）
- bored / boring（无聊的）
- surprised / surprising（惊讶的）
- moved / moving（感动的）
- tired / tiring（疲惫的）
- disappointed / disappointing（失望的）

**规律**：-ed修饰人（人的感受），-ing修饰物（物的特征）

---

### 2. alone vs lonely

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| alone | 独自（客观状态，不带感情） | He lives alone. |
| lonely | 孤独的（主观感受） | He feels lonely. |

**记忆口诀**：alone只独自，lonely心孤独

---

### 3. hard vs hardly

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| hard | adj./adv. | 困难的/努力地 | work hard（努力工作） |
| hardly | adv. | 几乎不 | I can hardly hear you.（我几乎听不见） |

**同类词**：
- late（迟的）vs lately（最近）
- near（近的）vs nearly（几乎）
- high（高的）vs highly（高度地）
- deep（深的）vs deeply（深深地）

---

### 4. other vs another vs the other vs others vs the others

| 词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| other | adj.+n.复数（其他的） | other students |
| another | 三者及以上中的另一个 | another cup of tea |
| the other | 两者中的另一个 | one... the other... |
| others | =other+n.复数 | Some like tea, others like coffee. |
| the others | 特指其余的 | Three are red, the others are blue. |

---

### 5. farther vs further

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| farther | 更远（距离） | He lives farther than me. |
| further | 进一步（程度/抽象） | further discussion / further study |

---

## 三、名词辨析

### 1. job vs work

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| job | 可数名词 | 具体的工作/职业 | I got a new job. / What's your job? |
| work | 不可数名词 | 工作（泛指） | I have a lot of work to do. |

---

### 2. chance vs opportunity

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| chance | 机会（偶然性）/可能性 | by chance（偶然）/ There's a chance of rain. |
| opportunity | 机会（有利条件） | a good opportunity to learn |

---

### 3. journey vs travel vs trip vs tour

| 词 | 含义 | 用法 |
|---|------|------|
| journey | 长途旅行 | a long journey |
| travel | 旅行（泛指） | travel abroad / travels（游记） |
| trip | 短途旅行 | a business trip / a school trip |
| tour | 观光旅行 | a tour of the city |

---

### 4. noise vs voice vs sound

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| noise | 噪音（不悦耳） | Don't make any noise. |
| voice | 人的声音/嗓音 | She has a beautiful voice. |
| sound | 声音（泛指） | the sound of music |

---

### 5. problem vs question

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|------|------|
| problem | 问题（需要解决） | solve a problem |
| question | 问题（需要回答） | ask a question / answer questions |

---

## 四、介词辨析

### 1. in vs on vs at（时间）

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| in | 年/月/季节/世纪/上下午 | in 2027 / in May / in spring / in the morning |
| on | 具体某一天/星期 | on Monday / on May 1st / on a rainy day |
| at | 具体时刻/节日 | at 8 o'clock / at noon / at Christmas |

---

### 2. in vs on vs at（地点）

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| in | 在...里面/大地点 | in the box / in China / in Beijing |
| on | 在...上面 | on the desk / on the wall |
| at | 在...处/小地点 | at the bus stop / at home / at school |

---

### 3. between vs among

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| between | 两者之间 | between you and me |
| among | 三者及以上之间 | among the students |

---

### 4. except vs besides vs except for

| 介词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|-----|------|
| except | 除...外（不包括） | Everyone is here except Tom. |
| besides | 除...外（还有/包括） | Besides English, he speaks French. |
| except for | 除...外（整体肯定，局部否定） | The essay is good except for some spelling mistakes. |

---

## 五、连词/连接词辨析

### 1. because vs because of vs due to vs owing to

| 词 | 词性 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| because | 连词 | +从句 | I stayed home because it rained. |
| because of | 介词短语 | +名词/代词/动名词 | I stayed home because of the rain. |
| due to | 介词短语 | +名词/动名词 | The delay was due to bad weather. |
| owing to | 介词短语 | +名词 | Owing to the rain, the match was cancelled. |

---

### 2. although vs though vs even though vs despite vs in spite of

| 词 | 词性 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|------|-----|------|
| although | 连词 | +从句（较正式） | Although it rained, we went out. |
| though | 连词 | +从句（可放句末） | It rained. We went out, though. |
| even though | 连词 | +从句（即使，语气更强） | Even though it rained hard, we went out. |
| despite | 介词 | +名词/动名词 | Despite the rain, we went out. |
| in spite of | 介词短语 | +名词/动名词 | In spite of the rain, we went out. |

---
