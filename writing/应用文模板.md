# 应用文写作模板

## 应用文概述

- **分值**：15分
- **字数**：80-100词
- **时间**：建议15分钟

---

## 一、通用格式

### 书信格式

```
Dear + 称呼,
    （正文，首行缩进4个字母）
    第一段：开门见山，说明写信目的
    第二段：具体内容
    第三段：表达期待/感谢

                                    Yours sincerely/truly,
                                    Li Hua
```

### 通知格式

```
                        NOTICE/ANNOUNCEMENT
    （正文）

                                    Student Union / The Committee
                                    日期（June 1, 2027）
```

## 二、各类书信模板

### 1. 建议信 (Letter of Suggestion)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to offer some suggestions about...
- I'm glad to hear that... and I'd like to share some advice.
- Learning that you are..., I'm writing to give you some suggestions.

**建议表达**：
- I would strongly recommend that you (should)...
- It would be a good idea if you could...
- Why not...? / How about...?
- I suggest that you (should)...
- You might as well...

**结尾**：
- I hope you will find these suggestions helpful.
- I do hope my suggestions can be of some help to you.
- I believe you will take my advice into consideration.

### 2. 邀请信 (Letter of Invitation)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to invite you to...
- I wonder if you would like to...
- We would be honored if you could join us...

**活动介绍**：
- The activity will be held at... on...
- There will be various activities, such as...
- It is a great opportunity for you to...

**结尾**：
- I sincerely hope you can accept my invitation.
- Would you please let me know as soon as possible if you can come?
- I'm looking forward to your early reply.

### 3. 感谢信 (Letter of Thanks)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to express my sincere gratitude to you for...
- I would like to take this opportunity to thank you for...
- Words fail to express my heartfelt thanks for...

**具体感谢**：
- It was so kind/thoughtful of you to...
- Without your help, I couldn't have...
- I really appreciate everything you have done for me.

**结尾**：
- Thank you again for your generous help.
- I will always remember your kindness.
- Please accept my most sincere thanks.

### 4. 申请信 (Letter of Application)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to apply for the position of.../the opportunity to...
- I learned from... that you are looking for...
- I'm interested in... and I'd like to apply for it.

**自我介绍**：
- I am a... student from...
- I have a strong interest in...
- I have relevant experience in...
- My advantages are as follows: First... Second... Third...

**结尾**：
- I would appreciate it if you could give me the opportunity.
- I'm confident that I can do the job well.
- I'm looking forward to your favorable reply.

### 5. 道歉信 (Letter of Apology)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to apologize for...
- I feel terribly sorry for...
- Please accept my sincere apology for...

**解释原因**：
- I was unable to... because...
- The reason is that...
- I had no choice but to...

**补救措施**：
- To make up for it, I will...
- Could we arrange another time?
- I promise it won't happen again.

**结尾**：
- Once again, please accept my apology.
- I hope you can forgive me.
- I sincerely hope this has not caused you much inconvenience.

### 6. 求助信 (Letter of Asking for Help)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to ask for your help with...
- I wonder if you could do me a favor.
- I'm having trouble with... and I need your help.

**说明困难**：
- I have difficulty (in) doing...
- What troubles me most is...
- I find it hard to...

**请求帮助**：
- I would be grateful if you could...
- Could you please give me some advice on...?
- Would it be possible for you to...?

**结尾**：
- I would really appreciate your help.
- Thank you in advance for your help.
- I'm looking forward to your reply.

### 7. 投诉信 (Letter of Complaint)

**开头**：
- I'm writing to complain about...
- I'm writing to express my dissatisfaction with...
- I regret to inform you that...

**说明问题**：
- The problem is that...
- I was disappointed to find that...
- It has caused me great inconvenience.

**要求解决**：
- I insist that you should...
- I demand a full refund / an immediate solution.
- I hope you will take effective measures to...

**结尾**：
- I'm looking forward to your prompt reply.
- I expect a satisfactory solution as soon as possible.

## 三、功能句型库

### 表示目的

- The purpose of this letter is to...
- I'm writing with the purpose of...
- I'm writing in the hope that...

### 表示希望

- I sincerely hope that...
- It is my sincere hope that...
- I do hope you will...

### 表示感谢

- Thank you for your time and consideration.
- I really appreciate your help.
- I'm grateful for your support.

### 表示期待

- I'm looking forward to your reply.
- I'm eagerly awaiting your response.
- I hope to hear from you soon.

## 四、常用词汇升级

| 基础词 | 高级替换 |
|--------|---------|
| help | assistance, aid, support |
| happy | delighted, thrilled, overjoyed |
| sad | disappointed, upset, heartbroken |
| good | excellent, outstanding, remarkable |
| bad | terrible, awful, disappointing |
| want | intend, desire, long for |
| think | consider, believe, hold the view that |
| important | significant, vital, essential |
| very | extremely, incredibly, tremendously |

---

## 五、连接词汇总

### 递进关系
Besides, Furthermore, Moreover, What's more, In addition

### 转折关系
However, Nevertheless, On the other hand, Instead

### 因果关系
Therefore, Thus, As a result, Consequently

### 举例说明
For example, For instance, Such as, Take...for example

### 总结
In conclusion, To sum up, In brief, All in all
