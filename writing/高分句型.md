# 高分句型积累

## 一、写作万能句型

### 1. 开篇句型

**表示目的**
- I'm writing to express my views on... (我写信是为了表达我对...的看法)
- The purpose of this letter is to... (这封信的目的是...)
- I am writing in response to... (我写信是为了回应...)

**表示原因**
- The reason why I'm writing to you is that... (我写信给你的原因是...)
- I'm writing to you because... (我写信给你是因为...)

**引出话题**
- When it comes to..., people hold different opinions. (谈到...，人们有不同的看法)
- With the development of..., ... has become a hot topic. (随着...的发展，...已成为热门话题)
- There is no denying that... (不可否认...)

### 2. 过渡句型

**递进**
- What's more / Moreover / Furthermore / Besides / In addition (此外)
- Not only... but also... (不仅...而且...)
- More importantly / What matters most is that... (更重要的是)

**转折**
- However / Nevertheless / Nonetheless (然而)
- On the contrary / On the other hand (相反/另一方面)
- Despite / In spite of (尽管)

**因果**
- As a result / Consequently / Therefore / Thus (因此)
- Due to / Owing to / Thanks to (由于)
- That's why... / That's the reason why... (这就是为什么)

**举例**
- For example / For instance (例如)
- Take... for example (以...为例)
- A case in point is... (一个典型的例子是)

### 3. 结尾句型

**总结**
- In conclusion / To sum up / In brief / All in all (总之)
- To put it in a nutshell (简而言之)
- From what has been discussed above, we can safely draw the conclusion that... (从以上讨论，我们可以得出结论)

**期待**
- I'm looking forward to your reply. (期待你的回复)
- I sincerely hope that... (我真诚地希望...)
- I would appreciate it if you could... (如果你能...，我将不胜感激)

## 二、语法高分句型

### 1. 强调句

**It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分**

- It was my mother who/that taught me the importance of honesty.
  (正是我的母亲教会了我诚实的重要性)
- It is through hard work that we can achieve success.
  (正是通过努力工作我们才能取得成功)
- It was not until I failed the exam that I realized the importance of study.
  (直到我考试不及格，我才意识到学习的重要性)

### 2. 倒装句

**否定词在句首**
- Never have I seen such a beautiful scenery.
  (我从未见过如此美丽的风景)
- Not until then did I realize my mistake.
  (直到那时我才意识到我的错误)
- Hardly had I arrived home when it began to rain.
  (我刚到家就开始下雨)

**So/Such...that结构**
- So moved was I that tears filled my eyes.
  (我如此感动以至于眼中充满了泪水)
- So beautiful is the scenery that I can't help taking photos.
  (风景如此美丽以至于我忍不住拍照)

**Only + 状语在句首**
- Only in this way can we solve the problem.
  (只有这样我们才能解决问题)
- Only when I grew up did I understand my parents' love.
  (只有当我长大后，我才理解父母的爱)

### 3. 非谓语动词结构

**分词作状语**
- Hearing the news, she burst into tears. (听到这个消息，她哭了起来)
- Faced with difficulties, we should never give up. (面对困难，我们不应该放弃)
- Deeply moved by his story, I decided to help him. (被他的故事深深打动，我决定帮助他)

**独立主格结构**
- Weather permitting, we will go for a picnic. (天气允许的话，我们会去野餐)
- Time passing quickly, we have to cherish every moment. (时间过得很快，我们要珍惜每一刻)
- All things considered, the plan is practical. (考虑到所有因素，这个计划是可行的)

### 4. 定语从句

**非限制性定语从句**
- My mother, who is a teacher, always encourages me to study hard.
  (我的母亲是一名教师，她总是鼓励我努力学习)
- The book, which was written by Mo Yan, is very popular.
  (这本书是莫言写的，非常受欢迎)

**介词+关系代词**
- This is the house in which I was born.
  (这就是我出生的房子)
- The teacher from whom I learned a lot has retired.
  (那位让我学到很多的老师退休了)

### 5. 名词性从句

**主语从句**
- What impressed me most was his patience.
  (给我印象最深的是他的耐心)
- It is widely acknowledged that education is important.
  (人们普遍认为教育很重要)

**表语从句**
- The fact is that we have no choice.
  (事实是我们别无选择)
- That's why I am writing to you.
  (这就是我写信给你的原因)

### 6. 虚拟语气

**If虚拟条件句**
- If I were you, I would accept the offer.
  (如果我是你，我会接受这个提议)
- If I had worked harder, I would have passed the exam.
  (如果我当时更努力，我就会通过考试)

**wish引导的虚拟**
- I wish I could fly like a bird.
  (我希望我能像鸟一样飞翔)
- I wish I had taken your advice.
  (我真希望我当时听了你的建议)

## 三、情感描写句型

### 积极情感

- A wave of happiness swept over me.
  (一阵幸福感涌上心头)
- I was overwhelmed with joy/excitement.
  (我兴奋/喜悦得不知所措)
- My heart was filled with gratitude.
  (我的心中充满了感激)
- Tears of joy rolled down my cheeks.
  (喜悦的泪水顺着脸颊流下)
- I couldn't contain my excitement.
  (我抑制不住自己的兴奋)

### 消极情感

- My heart sank at the news.
  (听到这个消息，我的心沉了下去)
- A sense of disappointment washed over me.
  (一股失望感涌上心头)
- I was seized by fear.
  (我被恐惧攫住了)
- Tears welled up in my eyes.
  (泪水涌上眼眶)
- My spirits sank to rock bottom.
  (我的情绪跌到了谷底)

## 四、环境/场景描写句型

- The sun was shining brightly, casting golden light over everything.
  (阳光明媚，金色的光芒洒满大地)
- A gentle breeze was blowing, carrying the fragrance of flowers.
  (微风轻拂，带来了花香)
- Dark clouds gathered overhead, and thunder rumbled in the distance.
  (乌云密布，远处雷声隆隆)
- The leaves rustled in the wind, creating a peaceful atmosphere.
  (树叶在风中沙沙作响，营造出宁静的氛围)
- Silence hung heavy in the air.
  (空气中弥漫着沉重的寂静)

- Practice makes perfect. (熟能生巧)
- A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. (千里之行，始于足下)
- No pains, no gains. (不劳无获)
- Knowledge is power. (知识就是力量)
- Time and tide wait for no man. (时不我待)
- Every coin has two sides. (事物都有两面性)
