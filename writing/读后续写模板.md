# 读后续写模板与技巧

## 读后续写概述

- **分值**：25分
- **字数**：150词左右（两段，每段约75词）
- **时间**：建议25-30分钟

---

## 一、首句分析技巧

### 1. 分析要点

- **时态**：确定续写使用的主要时态
- **人物**：明确主要人物及其关系
- **情节走向**：从首句推断可能的发展方向
- **情感基调**：积极/消极/转折

### 2. 首句类型

| 首句类型 | 特点 | 续写方向 |
|----------|------|----------|
| 动作首句 | 描写某个动作 | 顺承动作结果 |
| 对话首句 | 引用人物对话 | 回应对话内容 |
| 情感首句 | 描写心理感受 | 解释原因或转变 |
| 场景首句 | 描写环境场景 | 结合场景推进 |

---

## 二、情节发展套路

### 1. 常见情节模式

**模式一：困境→解决→感悟**
- Para 1：遇到困难/冲突，尝试解决
- Para 2：问题解决，收获感悟

**模式二：误解→真相→和解**
- Para 1：误解加深，情感冲突
- Para 2：了解真相，重归于好

**模式三：挑战→成功→成长**
- Para 1：面对挑战，付出努力
- Para 2：获得成功，自我成长

### 2. 情节发展连贯词

| 功能 | 连接词 |
|------|--------|
| 时间顺序 | Then, After that, Moments later, The next moment |
| 突然转折 | Suddenly, All of a sudden, To one's surprise |
| 同时进行 | Meanwhile, At the same time |
| 结果 | Consequently, As a result, Therefore |
| 最终 | Eventually, Finally, In the end |

## 三、描写技巧

### 1. 情感描写

#### 积极情感

| 情感 | 表达方式 |
|------|----------|
| 高兴 | A wave of joy swept over me. / I was overwhelmed with happiness. |
| 激动 | My heart raced with excitement. / I could barely contain my excitement. |
| 感激 | A surge of gratitude welled up in my heart. / Tears of gratitude rolled down my cheeks. |
| 自豪 | A sense of pride swelled in my chest. / I was filled with pride. |
| 感动 | I was deeply touched/moved. / My heart melted at the sight. |

#### 消极情感

| 情感 | 表达方式 |
|------|----------|
| 悲伤 | Tears welled up in my eyes. / My heart sank. |
| 害怕 | Fear seized me. / My heart pounded with terror. |
| 失望 | A wave of disappointment washed over me. / My spirits sank. |
| 紧张 | My heart was in my mouth. / I felt butterflies in my stomach. |
| 愤怒 | Anger boiled up inside me. / I was consumed with anger. |

### 2. 动作描写

#### 基础动作升级

| 基础 | 高级表达 |
|------|----------|
| run | dash, rush, sprint, race |
| walk | stroll, stride, pace, wander |
| say | whisper, murmur, exclaim, declare |
| cry | sob, weep, burst into tears |
| look | glance, gaze, stare, glare |
| hold | clutch, grip, grasp, embrace |

#### 细节动作链

不要：He opened the door and went in.

要：He reached out his trembling hand, grasped the cold door handle, turned it slowly, and pushed the door open inch by inch.

### 3. 环境描写

#### 烘托心情

| 心情 | 环境描写 |
|------|----------|
| 愉快 | The sun was shining brightly and birds were singing merrily. |
| 悲伤 | Dark clouds gathered overhead and a cold wind began to blow. |
| 紧张 | The silence was deafening, broken only by the pounding of my heart. |
| 希望 | A ray of sunshine broke through the clouds. |

## 四、高分句型

### 1. 倒装句

- **So + adj. + be + 主语 + that...**
  So excited was I that I could hardly speak.

- **Not until... did...**
  Not until I saw her smile did I realize my effort was worthwhile.

### 2. 强调句

- **It was... that...**
  It was at that moment that I understood the true meaning of friendship.

### 3. 分词结构

- **现在分词作状语**
  Hearing the good news, she jumped with joy.

- **过去分词作状语**
  Deeply moved by his words, I couldn't help crying.

### 4. with复合结构

- With tears streaming down her face, she hugged me tightly.
- With his hands trembling, he opened the envelope.

### 5. 独立主格结构

- The task completed, we all breathed a sigh of relief.
- Heart pounding, I knocked on the door.

### 6. 名词性从句

- What struck me most was his selfless dedication.
- Little did I know that this experience would change my life forever.

## 五、结尾升华模板

### 1. 感悟类结尾

- From that day on, I learned that... (从那天起，我明白了...)
- Looking back, I realized that... (回顾往事，我意识到...)
- This experience taught me that... (这次经历教会了我...)

### 2. 情感类结尾

- A warm feeling spread through my heart. (一股温暖涌上心头)
- At that moment, I was overwhelmed with gratitude. (那一刻，我感激不已)
- Tears of joy streamed down my face. (喜悦的泪水顺着脸庞流下)

### 3. 展望类结尾

- I knew that this was just the beginning of... (我知道这只是...的开始)
- From then on, I determined to... (从那以后，我决心...)
- With a new sense of purpose, I... (带着新的目标，我...)

## 六、万能句式库

### 开头承接

1. No sooner had I/he... than... (我/他一...就...)
2. Hardly had I/he... when... (我/他刚...就...)
3. At the sight of..., I/he... (一看到...，我/他...)
4. The moment I/he..., ... (我/他一...，就...)

### 过渡连接

1. What happened next took me by surprise. (接下来发生的事让我大吃一惊)
2. To my relief/disappointment/surprise, ... (让我松了口气/失望/惊讶的是)
3. It suddenly occurred to me that... (我突然想到...)
4. An idea flashed through my mind. (一个想法闪过我的脑海)

### 情节推进

1. Without hesitation, I/he... (毫不犹豫地，我/他...)
2. Gathering all my courage, I... (鼓起所有勇气，我...)
3. Taking a deep breath, I... (深吸一口气，我...)
4. Determined to..., I/he... (决心要...，我/他...)

## 七、常见主题素材

### 1. 友情类

关键词：misunderstanding, reconciliation, trust, loyalty, support

### 2. 家庭类

关键词：love, sacrifice, gratitude, reunion, understanding

### 3. 成长类

关键词：challenge, perseverance, confidence, self-discovery, breakthrough

### 4. 助人类

关键词：kindness, compassion, volunteer, impact, gratitude

### 5. 自然类

关键词：adventure, survival, courage, teamwork, respect for nature

## 八、检查清单

写完后检查：

- [ ] 是否紧扣给定首句
- [ ] 情节是否连贯合理
- [ ] 是否有情感描写
- [ ] 是否使用高级词汇和句型
- [ ] 时态是否统一
- [ ] 字数是否合适（约150词）
- [ ] 是否有拼写和语法错误
- [ ] 结尾是否有升华
